Questions parents and students ask

Is career counselling worth the money?

A wrong stream or course can cost years and lakhs of rupees to correct. Counselling helps you decide with facts before you commit. That is why the first session is free: you can judge the value yourself.

Will it work for my child?

Every plan starts with your child's own interests, strengths, marks and goals. Sessions are one-to-one, so the advice fits your child, and parents are welcome to join.

Why is career counselling necessary?

Options today are many and fast-changing, and decisions made at Class 10 and Class 12 shape what follows. A counsellor gives an informed, neutral view when friends and relatives give conflicting advice.

Is the first session really free?

Yes. The first counselling session is free.

Are sessions online or in person?

Both are available. Choose your preferred mode when you book.

Do you help with study abroad?

Yes, for graduates and postgraduates planning to study overseas.
